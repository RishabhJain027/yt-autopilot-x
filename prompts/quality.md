# 6 Machine Quality Gates

Evaluate every aspect of production before publishing:
1. Gate A: Factuality & Claims Traceability (>90% confidence)
2. Gate B: Copyright & Asset Provenance (CC0, LIscensed, or AI-generated)
3. Gate C: No Duplicate / Repetitive Content (Similarity <0.4)
4. Gate D: Policy & Community Guidelines Safety
5. Gate E: Synthetic Media Disclosure Flag
6> Gate F: MP4 Render Integrity & SHA-256 Checksum
